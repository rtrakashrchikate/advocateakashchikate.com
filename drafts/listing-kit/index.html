Long · about / bio
Advocate Akash R. Chikate is a criminal defence lawyer with his main office in Shivajinagar, Pune, close to the Pune District and Sessions Court. He is enrolled with the Bar Council of Maharashtra & Goa (MAH/4681/2020) and holds a B.A. LL.B. and an LL.M. in Science & Technology Laws from Savitribai Phule Pune University, with a Diploma in Cyber Laws. His practice covers anticipatory bail and regular bail, police notices under Section 35 BNSS, police custody and remand hearings, default bail, discharge applications, sessions trials, criminal appeals and suspension of sentence, and FIR quashing petitions at the Bombay High Court. He also handles cases under MCOCA, the MPID Act and EOW investigations, NDPS, POCSO, the SC/ST Act, Section 498A / Section 85 BNS, cheque bounce and cyber crime, including bank accounts frozen by cyber police. Clients come from across Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad; a second office at Pimple Gurav is open by appointment. Consultations are in English, Hindi and Marathi. Office hours: Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 7 pm.
