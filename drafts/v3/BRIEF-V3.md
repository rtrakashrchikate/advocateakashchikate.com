## Lessons from the last batch (apply them)
- Section numbers: verify against the bare act text (BNSS/BNS/BSA Gazette PDFs or India Code), not only a comparison table. Known corrections already found: cancellation of bail is Section 480(5) / 483(3) BNSS, NOT Section 484 (that is bond amount); Section 480 BNSS covers Magistrates, not the Sessions Court or High Court; BNS Section 105 is culpable homicide not amounting to murder (103 is murder).
- Judgments: the earlier pages relied on news summaries. For THIS batch, open the judgment itself (sci.gov.in PDF, indiankanoon, or the High Court order) and cite only what you read: case name, court, date, bench, and the point decided in your own words. Fewer solid citations beat many half-checked ones. If you cannot open the judgment, leave it out.
- Date today: 17 November 2026. "Recent" means 2025 to 2026. Do not cite anything you cannot confirm exists.
- Name no state other than Maharashtra in the body text; case names that contain another state are fine (State of X v. Y).
- Rule 36: no "best/top/expert", no results, no testimonials, no fee amounts. Describe what courts decided, never what the advocate achieved.
