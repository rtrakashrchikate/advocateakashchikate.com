Maharashtra · FIR guide

Is there an FIR
against you?

Check it online on the Maharashtra Police portal in three steps.

एफआयआर ऑनलाइन कशी तपासायची

1

Open “Search & View Published FIR” on citizen.mahapolice.gov.in

2

Enter a date range (under 90 days) and choose the unit and police station

3

Open the FIR and save it as a PDF. Sensitive cases are not published online.
