Supreme Court of India · Your rights

Arrested? Ask for the grounds in writing.

The Supreme Court has held that the grounds of arrest must be given in writing, in a language the person understands, for every offence.

Mihir Rajesh Shah v. State of Maharashtra, decided 6 November 2025
If written grounds cannot be given at once, they must be given at least two hours before remand
Without them, the arrest and remand can be challenged as illegal
