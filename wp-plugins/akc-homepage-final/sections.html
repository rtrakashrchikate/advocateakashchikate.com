What to expect

How a matter usually proceeds

  1. Step 1

    Consultation

    You explain the situation and share the documents you have, in person, by phone or on video.

  2. Step 2

    Reading the record

    The FIR, orders and papers are read, and your legal position and options are explained.

  3. Step 3

    Filing and hearing

    The application or reply is drafted, filed in the right court and argued at the hearing.

  4. Step 4

    Order and next steps

    The order is explained, with the conditions to follow and the stage that comes next.
