# Keyword gap plan: new pages to build

**Date:** 25 October 2026 · **Method:** compared the site's 149 URLs against high-intent Pune/Maharashtra legal searches, then checked live results for the gaps. There was no Search Console, DataForSEO or volume data, so priority is judged on intent, local fit and how weak the current results are, not on search volume. The search tool returned US results, so treat the competition read as directional.

## Where the site already wins
Bail, anticipatory bail, FIR quashing, NDPS, 498A (husband's side), cybercrime and bank freezes, EOW, MCOCA, POCSO, SC/ST Act, default bail, discharge, mutual divorce, custody, consumer court. These are well covered in English and Marathi. New work should go into the gaps below, not more pages on these.

## Page template (what made the existing pages work)
- Title: `<Problem> in Pune: <what to do / section>`, at most 60 characters, with old and new section numbers where people still search the old one.
- Opening 2–3 sentences answer the question directly (good for AI answers and featured snippets).
- Sections: what it is → what happens next and timelines → your options (Sessions Court / High Court) → documents → realistic cost and time → FAQ (5–6 questions).
- A Marathi twin for #1, #2, #3 and #10 (Pune searches use Marathi for police matters), linked with hreflang through the existing system.
- Internal links: each new page linked from 2–3 related practice-area pages and the matching `/criminal-lawyer/<area>/` pages; add new topics to `llms.txt`.

## Suggested order
1. Tadipar (+ Marathi), 2. MPDA, 3. Hit-and-run, 4. False rape / Section 69, 5. Maintenance (+ Marathi), then the rest. Re-check rankings 6–8 weeks after publishing; ideally with Search Console connected.
