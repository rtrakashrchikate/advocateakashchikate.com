## Ad wording rules (BCI Rule 36 + Google policy)

- Factual and informational only: what the process is, which court, documents, timelines.
- **Never:** "best", "top", "No. 1", "expert", "guaranteed", "100% success", win rates, case results, client testimonials, fees, discounts, "call now and get bail".
- No phone number in ad text (use the call asset). No exclamation marks, no clickbait.
- Example for Anticipatory bail (headlines ≤30, descriptions ≤90 characters):
  - Headlines: `Anticipatory Bail in Pune` · `Process, Documents, Timeline` · `Sessions Court & High Court` · `Advocate Akash R. Chikate` · `Office in Shivajinagar, Pune`
  - Descriptions: `What anticipatory bail is, when to apply and the documents needed. Read the guide.` · `Bombay High Court and Pune District Courts. Consultation by appointment.`
