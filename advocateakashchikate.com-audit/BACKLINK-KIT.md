# Backlink kit: advocateakashchikate.com

**Date:** 13 November 2026. **Source checked:** Nick Nolan's list of 48 "free do-follow" sites (Medium, May 2025).

## Verdict on that list

Most of it is no use to a criminal lawyer in Pune. These are profile links on sites like Redbubble, Giphy, JetPhotos and Parler. Google knows these are self-made and gives them almost no weight. A dozen of them on a law site can look like link spam. Profile links won't move you from position 10 to the top 3 for "criminal lawyer pune". What does that is links and mentions from Pune and legal sources (section C).

Worth doing from the list: **6 sites** (section A). Skip the rest.

**How to do it safely:** no more than 3–4 profiles a day, use a different description on each (three versions are below), and use the same name, address and phone everywhere.

## C. The links that actually move rankings (slower, highest value)

1. **Legal news sites:** LiveLaw, Bar & Bench, LawBeat, SCC Online blog. Send a short write-up of a reported Bombay High Court or Pune Sessions Court order you appeared in (only orders that are already public).
2. **Pune news:** Pune Mirror, Punekar News, Hindustan Times Pune, Lokmat. Offer to comment on digital arrest scams, frozen bank accounts or tadipar notices; your guides already cover these.
3. **Pune Bar Association and the Bombay High Court bar directories.**
4. **Your universities:** SPPU / your LLM institute alumni pages.
5. **Rotaract or any club you belong to:** a member profile with a website link.

## Why I can't create these accounts myself

Each site needs an account in your name, a verification email, often a captcha, and sometimes a phone OTP. Signing up for you would mean using your email and phone, and accounts created by automation in bulk are exactly what Google and these sites flag as spam. The safe way is for you to make 3–4 a day from your phone, pasting from this page. It takes about 5 minutes each.
