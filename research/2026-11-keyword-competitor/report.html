Pune Defence Search Research

What people in Pune search when they need bail, anticipatory bail or FIR quashing, what worries them, how competing lawyers win those searches, and what we do next for advocateakashchikate.com.

Sources: your Google Search Console (last 90 days), 2,474 Google search-suggestion lookups in English, Hindi and Marathi (5,738 phrases kept), about 60 forum searches with 24 threads read (LawRato, LawyersClubIndia, Kaanoon, Quora headings), and a teardown of 10 competitor websites.

What we found

Five findings decide the plan.

1
Fee and "how many days" searches are your closest-to-hiring traffic, and you already rank for them.

"fir quashing fees" and similar phrases sent your quashing guide about 600 Google views in 90 days at position about 6, but only 4 clicks. The page appears but does not answer the cost question in the search result. Hindi phrases like अग्रिम जमानत में कितना पैसा लगता है and हाई कोर्ट से जमानत कितने दिन में होती है (184 views) have no page of their own.

2
Your hiring pages don't say "lawyer".

Your anticipatory bail page is titled "Anticipatory Bail in Pune: Meaning, Process, Section 482" and the quashing page "Quashing of FIR in Bombay High Court". Competitors that rank use "Anticipatory Bail Lawyer in Pune". You are at about 31 for "anticipatory bail lawyer pune" and about 36 for "criminal lawyer in pune".

3
Reviews and directory listings are the gap, not content.

The strongest local competitor shows "5.0 on Google · 351+ reviews" on every page; you have 20 and show them nowhere on your site. Directories (mostly LawRato) hold most page-1 places for "criminal lawyer Pune" type searches, and we could not find you on LawRato, RestTheCase or ThreeBestRated.

4
Two large groups of searches have no good page anywhere on your site.

Bank accounts frozen by cyber police (60+ distress phrases such as "account freeze ho gaya kya kare", "kitne din me khulta hai", freeze application format) and Pune courts (260+ phrases: which court hears a police station's cases, Shivajinagar and Morwadi court timings, case status).

5
Clients search with old section numbers and fear unclear fees.

Forum posters write "41A", "498a 406 34", "420/120b", not BNS numbers. Their most common complaints about lawyers: no clear fee, advance taken with no work, no copy of orders, missed dates. Trust words in reviews: "explained every document", "calm", "command over bail law".

What clients worry about

From public legal Q&A forums. Reddit could not be read from this environment, so these come from LawRato, LawyersClubIndia, Kaanoon and Quora question titles. Most posters were outside Pune.

What makes them search right now

  • Police keep phoning, but no written notice has come.
  • A 41A / 35(3) notice arrived, though "my name is not in the FIR".
  • Called to the Bharosa Cell or women's cell for counselling.
  • The wife's complaint names parents and sister too.
  • An NRI has just landed and fears arrest; job, visa or passport at risk.
  • Bank account frozen with no notice; the bank only says "police".
  • Sessions court rejected anticipatory bail: "will I be arrested on the spot?"

What they say about lawyers

  • The fee was never stated clearly, or grew later.
  • Advance paid, nothing done for months.
  • No copy of the order; missed hearing dates.
  • Fear of "money for the judge or police" demands.
  • Trust comes from being explained every document, calmness, and clear command of bail law.
Forum fee figures (anecdotal, mostly 2016 to 2024): anticipatory bail most often ₹20,000 to ₹50,000; Bombay High Court quashing from about ₹40,000 to ₹50,000, some up to ₹1.5 lakh. Recent Bombay High Court quashing orders after settlement have also imposed costs on the parties.
