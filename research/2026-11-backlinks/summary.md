## Data limits
- DataForSEO's index is smaller than Google's and misses many directory profiles, JS-rendered pages and nofollow social links. **None of the big Indian legal directories (Lawrato, Justdial, Sulekha, Vakilsearch, LegalKart, Kaanoon, AdvocateKhoj, LawyersClubIndia) appear in any of the 10 profiles.** That says more about index coverage than about where these lawyers are listed. Treat those directories as standard citations to claim anyway; this data neither confirms nor rules them out.
- Low-spam lists were fetched in full. High-spam domains (>40) were counted, not listed, except for Dhende and your own site, which were fetched in full.
- Raw files in raw/ are compacted (selected fields), not the verbatim API JSON.
- A linking page is shown only where seo.backlinks_backlinks returned one. Some links (pathlegal.in, tribuneindia.com, storeboard.com, goodfirms.co) were not returned, probably because they are lost or old.
- Signup URLs were not verified. Where a step says 'search ...', use that search.
- Compliance: Bar Council of India Rule 36 restricts advocates from soliciting work or advertising. Factual directory profiles are generally tolerated. 'Best lawyer' press releases and paid features carry risk.
